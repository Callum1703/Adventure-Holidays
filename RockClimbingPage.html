Rock Climbing

Rock climbing offers an exhilarating blend of physical challenge and mental strategy as
participants navigate vertical terrain using strength, flexibility, and problem-solving skills.
The activity typically begins with a safety briefing covering proper equipment use,
including harnesses, ropes, helmets, and climbing shoes, followed by instruction on basic climbing
techniques such as three-point contact, body positioning, and efficient movement.
Climbers can experience various formats including top-roping (where the rope runs through an anchor at the
top of the route), bouldering (low-height climbing without ropes over safety mats).
This activity is incredibly fun and challenging and can train your body to an incredible extent.

Itinerary

Pre Activity (1 - 2 days before)

Figure out what equipment you need to bring versus what's provided
Below is things you should pack

  • Comfortable athletic clothes
  • Water Bottle (at least 1 litre)
  • Small Snacks
  • Towel
  • Hand Lotion (for after climbing)

Day Of The Climb

8:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Preparation

9:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Arrival at Climbing Facility

9:30 AM - 10:15 AM: Intro and Safety Orientation

10:15 AM - 11:00 AM: Basic Techniques Lesson

11:00 AM - 12:00 AM: First Climbing Attempts

12:00 AM - 12:45 AM: Lunch Break

12:45 AM - 1:15 PM: Review and Technique Refinement

1:15 PM - 2:30 PM: Continued Practice

2:30 PM - 3:00 PM: CoolDown and Debrief

3:00 PM - 3:30 PM: Wrap Up
