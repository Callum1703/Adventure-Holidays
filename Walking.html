Walking

Walking is one of the simplest yet most effective forms of physical activity, providing numerous health
benefits for people of all ages.
It’s a low-impact exercise that can be done almost anywhere, whether in a park, on a treadmill, or around your neighborhood.
Regular walking helps improve cardiovascular health, strengthens muscles, and boosts mental well-being by reducing stress and anxiety.
It also promotes better circulation, aids in weight management, and improves flexibility and balance.
With no special equipment required, walking is an accessible way to stay active and enjoy the outdoors, making it an ideal activity for maintaining overall fitness.

Itinerary

Pre Activity (1 - 2 days before)

Figure out what equipment you need to bring versus what's provided
Below is a list of items you should pack

  • Comfortable athletic clothes (layers recommended)
  • Sturdy hiking or ankle-supporting shoes
  • Sunglasses
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Water bottle (at least 1 liter)
  • Light windbreaker/jacket
  • Camera
  • Walking Pole (Optional)
  • Hat or cap for sun protection
  • Small backpack for personal items
  • Blister plasters/first aid supplies
  • Trail Snacks
