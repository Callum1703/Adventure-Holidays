Itinerary

Pre Activity (1 - 2 days before)

Figure out what equipment you need to bring versus what's provided
Below is things you should pack

  • Comfortable athletic clothes (layers recommended)
  • Sturdy hiking or ankle-supporting shoes
  • Sunglasses (secured with strap)
  • Sunscreen and lip balm
  • Water bottle (at least 1 liter)
  • Light windbreaker/jacket
  • Camera (with secure attachment)
  • Gloves (lightweight)
  • Hat or cap (that fits under helmet)

Day Of The Paragliding Adventure

8:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Preparation

9:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Arrival at Paragliding Site

9:30 AM - 10:15 AM: Intro and Safety Orientation

10:15 AM - 11:00 AM: Ground Handling Lesson

11:00 AM - 12:00 AM: Initial Flight Preparation

12:00 AM - 12:45 AM: Lunch Break

12:45 AM - 1:15 PM: Pre-Flight Briefing

1:15 PM - 2:30 PM: Paragliding Flights

2:30 PM - 3:00 PM: CoolDown and Debrief

3:00 PM - 3:30 PM: Wrap Up
