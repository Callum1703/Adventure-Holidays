White Water Rafting

White water rafting is an exciting and exhilarating activity that offers numerous benefits.
It provides a great physical workout, engaging your core, arms, and legs as you paddle through fast-moving rapids.
The adrenaline rush it generates helps improve mental resilience and focus, encouraging participants to work as a team and make quick decisions under pressure.
Beyond the physical and mental benefits, white water rafting immerses you in the beauty of nature, allowing you to explore scenic rivers and remote landscapes in a thrilling way.
Whether you're an adrenaline junkie or someone seeking a unique outdoor adventure, white water rafting offers an unforgettable experience that strengthens bonds, enhances teamwork, and creates lasting memories.

Itinerary

Figure out what equipment you need to bring versus what's provided. Below is a list of things you should pack:

  • Quick-drying clothes (avoid cotton)
  • Swimsuit (if rafting attire isn't provided)
  • Waterproof footwear or sandals with straps
  • Hat or visor for sun protection
  • Waterproof sunscreen and lip balm
  • Waterproof camera or GoPro (optional)
  • Waterproof dry bag for personal items
  • Insect repellent
  • Personal medications (if necessary)
  • Extra set of clothes for after the trip
  • Snacks (energy bars, fruit, etc.)
  • Quick-dry towel

Day of the White Water Rafting Adventure

8:00 AM - 9:00 AM: Arrival at Rafting Base Camp

9:00 AM - 9:30 AM: Check-in and Gear Distribution

9:30 AM - 10:15 AM: Intro and Safety Orientation

10:15 AM - 11:00 AM: Paddling and Technique Tips

11:00 AM - 12:00 PM: First Rafting Section

12:00 PM - 12:45 PM: Lunch Break on the Riverbank

12:45 PM - 1:15 PM: Route Discussion and Safety Refresher

1:15 PM - 2:30 PM: Main Rafting Route (Exciting Rapids Ahead!)

2:30 PM - 3:00 PM: Cool Down and Stretch on the Shore

3:00 PM - 3:30 PM: Wrap-Up and Return to Base Camp
